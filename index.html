About

The cloud has a problem.
I fix it.

Most businesses don't have an AWS problem — they have an architecture problem. Misconfigured IAM roles, bloated EC2 instances, no monitoring, no plan. That's where I come in.

I'm trained in AWS Cloud Foundations and AWS Academy Architecting through the DEPI program, backed by a CompTIA Network+ certification and Cisco cybersecurity training. I understand the network layer that most cloud work sits on top of — and that grounding shows in how I design infrastructure.

Before cloud, I spent 2+ years working inside large enterprise environments — telecoms, financial services — where I learned how systems actually operate under pressure: data accuracy, compliance workflows, cross-team escalation. That's real-world context that doesn't come from a lab.

I'm currently pursuing my BSc in Computer Science (4th year, Future Academy Cairo) and actively expanding my AWS expertise. I take on freelance projects through Upwork, Fiverr, and Khamsat.
